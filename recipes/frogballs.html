Pickled Brussels Sprouts (Frog Balls).

Credit: Courtesy of Kelsey Riley.

Description:

Pickled Brussels sprouts, known as frog balls in my house, are crisp, tangy, and lightly spicy with garlic, peppercorns, and red pepper flakes. These quick refrigerator pickles require only a few hours of brining before they're ready for snacking or serving alongside sandwiches and charcuterie.

Ingredients:

  • 4 cups brussels sprouts.
  • 2 cups vinegar.
  • 2 cups water.
  • 2 cloves garlic.
  • 1 tablespoon kosher salt.
  • 1/2 teaspoon peppercorns.
  • 1/4 teaspoon crushed red pepper flakes.

Directions (Steps):

  1. Wash Brussels sprouts and trim off part of the hard ends but keep just enough so the entire sprout stays intact. Peel off 2 to 3 of the outer leaves.
  2. Fill a large bowl with ice and cold water. Bring a large pot of lightly salted water to a boil. Add Brussels sprouts and cook uncovered for 2 to 3 minutes. Drain in a colander and immediately immerse in ice water for 6 to 8 minutes to stop the cooking process. Drain.
  3. Combine water, vinegar, and salt in a pot for the brine. Set over medium heat and cook until salt has dissolved, 2 to 3 minutes.
  4. Evenly distribute Brussels sprouts, brine, garlic, peppercorns, and crushed red pepper flakes amongst several jars. Cover jars and refrigerate for at least 3 hours.

Nutritional Facts (Per Serving):

  • 41 Calories.
  • 6g Carbs.
  • 0g Fat.
  • 2g Protein.
