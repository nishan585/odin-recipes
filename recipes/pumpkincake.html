Pumpkin Gooey Butter Cake.

Credit: All Recipes.

Description:

Pumpkin gooey butter cake pairs a spiced pumpkin cake layer with a rich, creamy topping to make an irresistible fall dessert. Made with spice cake mix and canned pumpkin, it comes together with just 10 minutes of prep.

Ingredients:

  • 1 (13.25 to 15.25-ounce) package spice cake mix.
  • 1 (15-ounce) can pumpkin.
  • 1/2 cup butter, melted.
  • 4 large eggs, divided.
  • 2 teaspoons vanilla extract, divided.
  • 1 (8-ounce) package cream cheese.
  • 2 1/2 cups powdered sugar.

Directions (Steps):

  1. Preheat the oven to 350 degrees F (175 degrees C). Lightly grease a 9x13-inch baking dish.
  2. Combine cake mix, pumpkin, melted butter, 2 eggs, and 1 teaspoon vanilla extract in a large bowl. Beat with an electric mixer for 2 minutes. Spread mixture into the prepared baking dish.
  3. Add cream cheese to a bowl and beat with an electric mixer for 30 seconds. Add remaining 2 eggs and remaining 1 teaspoon vanilla extract. Beat until smooth. Slowly beat in powdered sugar. Pour cream cheese mixture evenly over the pumpkin layer.
  4. Bake until the top is lightly golden brown and the center is set, 35 to 40 minutes. Cool on a wire rack for 1 hour before serving.

Nutritional Facts (Per Serving):

  • 223 Calories.
  • 31g Carbs.
  • 10g Fat.
  • 3g Protein.
